# Example: builder-culture Reel

This is a routing example, not a collection of assets to reuse.

| Beat intent | Mode | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Product proof while the creator makes the comparison | A-roll + media minimal overlay | Keep the face visible; show the relevant launch/demo or product still raw, without a label beneath it. |
| Founder/build evidence | A-roll + media minimal overlay | Use real engineers, hardware assembly, or a satellite demo rather than a generic company photo. |
| A deliberate argument reset | White editorial canvas with captions | Centre a compact text stack on warm off-white; use no map, counter, or logo animation. |
| A spatial/company statistic | Motion graphics | Build the map, highlight, count, and logo row as separate visual steps. |
| The thesis becomes concrete | Full-screen B-roll | Use contiguous hard cuts between the robot, remote-control car, and AI toy; no A-roll flash between clips. |
| CTA or connective thought | A-roll + caption | Use lower-centre captions and the established emphasis colour. |

The soundtrack follows the same story logic: a short approved opening hook may repeat for the intro only, then the source continues naturally; the middle can duck or fade, and the ending should not become louder merely because the video is ending.
