# Full-screen B-roll

Use when the external footage should temporarily own the story.

- Let the clip fill the portrait frame with a deliberate crop or restrained push.
- Use the selected source in/out range from the asset manifest; do not pad a short moment with unrelated material.
- Consecutive B-roll clips must meet exactly on the timeline and use hard cuts by default.
- Pre-cut or pre-pad portrait media where necessary, but keep the visual itself clean.
- Use a poster or first-frame readiness fallback so the boundary never flashes A-roll, black, or an unready video frame.
- Keep source audio muted unless it is part of the approved sound design.
- Check the frame immediately before and after every cut.
