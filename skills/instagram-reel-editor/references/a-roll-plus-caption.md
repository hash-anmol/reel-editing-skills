# A-roll + caption

Use when the creator's face and delivery are the argument.

- Keep the A-roll full-frame and face-safe.
- Place captions in the lower-centre safe region, above platform UI.
- Use the established clean sans-serif caption identity with restrained orange or bold emphasis on one or two semantic words.
- Caption only the beats specified in the edit map; do not turn the whole Reel into continuous karaoke.
- Use word-timed reveals and subtle blur-settle or short vertical motion. Avoid bouncing, stickers, and unnecessary outlines.
- Captions must not collide with an overlay, hands, or the mouth. Verify at the actual frame, not only from CSS coordinates.
