# Grill Me Reel Edit

Use this skill when the creator has a source video, script, or rough idea and wants a professional editor's questions before production. It is a decision interview, not an editing or asset-download step.

## Before asking

Inspect the supplied files, previous reference edits, transcript, duration, and project state first. Do not ask the creator to identify a file, transcribe a clip, or research a fact that can be checked with available tools. Treat the spoken script as locked when the creator says not to change it; ask only about editorial treatment.

## Interview method

Work through the script in meaningful beats rather than interrogating every clause. Start with global decisions, then move to the beats whose treatment materially affects the edit:

1. Audience, platform, objective, pacing, and overall mood.
2. Caption and typography language.
3. Beat-level visual mode, face visibility, proof media, and transition.
4. Audio arc, music, sound effects, and silence.
5. Exceptions, attribution, fact risk, and call-to-action treatment.

For each question:

- Quote the relevant spoken line and its time range when available.
- Give a clear recommended default and one reason.
- Ask one decision at a time; do not bundle unrelated choices.
- Offer “use the default” as a valid answer.
- Separate editorial choices from facts and licensing; verify the latter yourself.

Do not begin implementation, downloading, or delegation during the interview. Stop when the material decisions are settled, summarize the choices, and ask for explicit approval of the decision summary.

## Output

Write `EDIT_DECISIONS.md` in the active project, including the script beat, decision, rationale, unresolved item, and approval status. This file is input to [`create-reel-editing-brief`](../create-reel-editing-brief/SKILL.md).

Read [`references/question-bank.md`](references/question-bank.md) only when a beat needs a prompt. Keep questions grounded in the current project; do not turn one reel's quirks into universal rules.
