# Reel edit question bank

Use only the questions that can change the cut. Present a recommendation first.

## Global

- What should the viewer feel or understand by the end?
- Should the creator remain the visual anchor, or should the evidence temporarily own the frame?
- Is the reference edit a source of principles (type, colour, pacing) or a frame-for-frame template?
- Which moments deserve stillness, and which deserve speed?

## Beat treatment

- Should this beat be **A-roll + caption**, **A-roll + minimal media overlay**, **full-screen B-roll**, **white editorial canvas with captions**, or **motion graphics**?
- What visual would prove the sentence rather than merely decorate it?
- Does the face need to remain visible while the media appears?
- Is the media allowed to crop, or must it be fully visible?
- Should the transition be a hard cut, a restrained dissolve, or a deliberate white reset?

## Text

- Is the spoken sentence itself captioned, or is this an authored title/card?
- Which one or two words deserve colour or weight?
- Should the text sit in the lower-centre safe zone or be centred as a full-screen statement?
- Is any label under the media genuinely needed? Default: no.

## Audio

- Is the music supporting dialogue, carrying a montage, or creating a transition?
- Does the source have a real continuation after the opening hook? Never loop a short phrase for the whole reel by default.
- Where should the bed fade, duck, pause, or return?
- Which sound effects clarify the edit, and which merely announce an edit?

## Approval

Summarize every settled choice as a short table and mark each one `approved`, `open`, or `rejected`. Do not start production while a material choice is still `open`.
